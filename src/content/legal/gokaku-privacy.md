**Short version: Gokaku collects nothing.** There are no accounts, no servers, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

## Data stored on your device

Gokaku saves your study progress — per-question statistics and mastery state —
your mock exam history, and your settings: theme, language, the Japanese-terms
toggle and batch size. On iOS this lives in `UserDefaults`, on Android in
`SharedPreferences`.

This data never leaves your device except in the two cases below. It is included
in your device backup only if you have enabled iCloud Backup or Google Backup
yourself; that is managed by your operating system, not by the app. You can erase
it at any time with **Settings → Reset progress**, or by deleting the app.

## Export and import

If you choose to export your progress, Gokaku writes a `gokaku-progress.json`
file and hands it to your operating system's share sheet. Import goes through the
system file picker. Where that file goes is entirely your choice — we never
receive it and the app never uploads it anywhere.

## Network access

The app itself makes no network requests. The only outbound traffic comes from
the operating system on your behalf: the App Store or Google Play when you make a
tip purchase, and the system "rate this app" prompt. Both are handled by Apple and
Google under their own privacy policies; no data from them reaches us.

## Payments

Tips are one-time in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never
see or store your payment details — only the store's aggregated reporting.

## Tracking and advertising

There is no App Tracking Transparency tracking, no advertising identifiers, no
analytics, no cookies and no profiling. The App Privacy label for Gokaku reads
**Data Not Collected**, and `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` declares
`NSPrivacyTracking = false` with empty collected-data and tracking-domain lists.

## Children

Gokaku is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access,
correct, export or delete on your behalf — you already control all of it, on your
device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
